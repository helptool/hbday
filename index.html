🌼Memory
Dear Sister,

Sometimes I wonder how fifteen years passed so quickly. It feels like yesterday when you were small enough to hold my hand while walking… and now you are growing into someone so beautiful, strong, and full of dreams.

Watching you grow has been one of the most emotional and beautiful journeys of my life. You are not just my sister. You are my comfort place, my happiness, my childhood, and one of the most precious parts of my heart.

Thank you for every laugh, every memory, every silly fight, every late-night conversation, and every single moment that made life feel brighter just because you were there.

I hope this new chapter brings you endless happiness, beautiful friendships, peaceful nights, unforgettable adventures, and dreams that finally come true.

And no matter where life takes us — always remember:

You will never walk alone.

I will always be proud of you. Always.

Your Brother 🤍
